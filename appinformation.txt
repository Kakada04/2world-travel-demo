PART I: COMPREHENSIVE WEB APPLICATION, UX & CMS AUDIT
1. Executive Summary
Business Profile: 2World Travel (Cambodia) Ltd is an accredited travel agency and tour operator established in Phnom Penh circa 2010 (Ministry of Commerce License So. 0021KH/2010; Ministry of Tourism License 098/11; CATA and PATA member) specializing in inbound multi-day tours, inter-city and cross-border transport ticketing, multilingual tour guide services, and corporate MICE logistics.
Application Architecture: Despite marketing an "own online reservation system," the existing website operates purely as an asynchronous manual lead-generation catalog and static directory. There is zero real-time inventory, no live payment gateway processing, and no instant booking engine.
Technical Degradation: The public frontend displays unrendered raw WordPress shortcodes across major landing pages, including [rev_slider alias="home1"], [tours_grid ...], [title ...], [metaslider id=4770], and [contact_info ...]. On the main Contact Us page, the primary inquiry form fails with the error message: Error: Contact form not found.
Content Stagnation & Decay: The site contains defunct airline listings (SilkAir, Dragonair), expired airfares ("Travel up to 28 February 2015"), outdated copyright notices (2009–2017), empty stub pages (e~VISA, CAMBODAI TOURS), and customer testimonials that end in December 2013.
Rebuild Necessity: A complete architectural overhaul is required, transitioning from the decaying monolithic WordPress theme to a modern headless/decoupled CMS architecture with strongly typed relational data models.

3. Current Business Features
Confirmed Operations: Inbound multi-day tour itineraries, Seat-in-Coach (SIC) group packages, niche tours (Bird Watching, Golf), flight ticketing, bus ticket consolidation (Giant Ibis, Mekong Express, Virak Buntham, Capitol, Soriya), Japan Rail & Vietnam rail ticket retail, certified multilingual guide hiring, MICE equipment rental, visa extensions/outbound processing, private vehicle rental, and travel insurance brokering (Forte Insurance).
Implied / Partially Implemented: Hotel booking engine (listed in navigation, but links are dead), e-Commerce checkout (WooCommerce artifact exists at /product/siem-reap-angkor-wat-3d-2n/, but inactive), e-Visa online processing (empty stub page).
Missing Travel Capabilities: Dynamic tour search/filtering, real-time availability calendar, local digital payments (Bakong KHQR, ABA PayWay), direct messaging widgets (Telegram/WhatsApp), multilingual support (Khmer/Chinese), and an automated CRM inquiry pipeline.
4. Page Template Analysis (7 Core Archetypes)
T1-Home (Homepage): Sticky topbar, hero banner slider, 4 quick services, tour highlights grid, 20-package matrix, 4-widget footer, and sidebar.
T2-TourListing (Tour Category): Breadcrumb, category hero, repeating tour cards (Title, Board Basis, Tour Code, "Read more…"), and sidebar.
T3-TourDetail (Itinerary Detail): Hero image, Introduction, In Brief summary, Day-by-Day blocks with meal codes ({B}, {L}, {D}), Inclusions/Exclusions list, Tour Code, and "Boook Now" CTA.
T4-TariffDirectory (Data & Tariff Tables): Dense multi-column tables for routes, operators, departure/arrival times, and foreigner/local fares with inquiry CTAs.
T5-ServiceInquiry (Interactive Service Form): Service descriptions, equipment rate tables, and multi-field quote/booking form.
T6-ContentEditorial (Destination & Cultural Guides): Long-form editorial layout with subheadings, image embeds, and informational sidebars.
T7-ContactHub (Contact & Reviews Portal): Multi-branch department directory (phone, email, Skype, office addresses), contact form, and customer review cards.
5. Reverse-Engineered CMS Entities (10 Core Types)
TourPackage: Core tour product (tour_code, title, slug, duration_days, duration_nights, board_basis, intro_text, inclusions, exclusions, featured_image).
ItineraryDay: Sub-entity of TourPackage (tour_id, day_number, title, narrative, meals_included, overnight_location).
AirlineDirectory: Carrier database (airline_name, iata_code, office_address, phone, email, website, is_active).
BusRoute: Transportation matrix (origin, destination, operator_name, departure_time, arrival_time, bus_type, fare_usd, frequency).
FlightFareQuote: Benchmark route pricing (departure_city, arrival_city, airline_partner, one_way_usd, round_trip_usd).
TourGuideRate: Professional guide tariffs (language, full_day_rate_usd, half_day_rate_usd, working_hours, policy).
VisaRequirement: Immigration rules (country_name, is_extension, visa_type, validity, processing_time, service_fee_usd, requirements).
MICEPackage & MICEEquipment: Event tariffs (package_number, rate_per_pax, daily_hall_rate, equipment rental items).
InquiryLead: Customer leads (lead_type, tour_code, client_name, client_email, client_phone, pax_count, travel_dates, status).
Testimonial: Client feedback (client_name, client_country, review_date, review_body, rating, is_approved).
6. Relational Data Model Schema
Normalizes relationships between TourPackage (1-to-many with ItineraryDay), Destination (many-to-many with TourPackage; 1-to-many with BusRoute origins/destinations), TourCategory, InquiryLead, and MICEPackage. Enforces unique constraints on tourCode to eliminate legacy copy-paste bugs.

9. Media & Digital Asset Audit
Logos: Low-resolution animated/static GIF (logo.gif) dating from 2017.
Sliders: Broken Revolution Slider and MetaSlider shortcode instances across templates.
Video: Embedded HTML5 MP4 player fails across all sidebars (Media error: Format(s) not supported).
Tour Photography: Unoptimized JPEGs lacking modern WebP/AVIF formats and responsive srcset breakpoints.
Recommendations: Implement a centralized Media Library with automated WebP conversion and offload video hosting to YouTube/Vimeo.
10. Technical Stack & Infrastructure Audit
Platform: Outdated WordPress installation on Apache/LiteSpeed shared hosting with cPanel.
Active / Failing Plugins: Revolution Slider, MetaSlider, Contact Form 7, WPCaptcha, WooCommerce (inactive artifact).
Security & Performance: Mixed-content warnings (assets loading over HTTP), missing modern security headers (HSTS, CSP), legacy Google Analytics (ga.js), lack of CDN caching.
11. Content Quality & Legacy Debt Audit
Defunct Airlines: Lists Dragonair (ceased operations in 2020) and SilkAir (merged into Singapore Airlines in 2021).
Expired Fares: Banners advertise airfares valid up to "28 February 2015".
Duplicate Tour Codes: 2WT-BC-10D9N is copied across Introducing Cambodia 6D5N and Grand Cambodia 19D18N.
Internal Itinerary Contradictions: Northeast Explorer heading mentions Siem Reap while text describes Phnom Penh and Kratie.
Conflicting Head Office Addresses: Mao Tse Toung Blvd (St. 245 corner of St. 199) vs. Borey Piphup Thmey II (St. BT12, Sangkat Pong Teuk, Khan Dangkor).
Spelling Typos: Joumey (page title), Boook Now (CTA buttons), Sreen Projector (form), Giant ibis Dalily (bus table).
12. SEO & Metadata Deficiencies
Title Tags: Duplicate titles used across different categories (e.g., standard Siem Reap tours and Golf tours both titled Siem Reap - 2worldtravel.com).
Meta Descriptions: Missing across subpages; search engines fall back to scraping raw navigation text.
Structured Data: Total absence of Schema.org JSON-LD (TravelAgency, TouristTrip, Product).
Images: Over 85% of images lack descriptive alt text.
