South Coast Explorer: Kampot, Kep & Koh Rong

Discover southern Cambodia's colonial riverside charm in Kampot, the famous Kep crab market and seaside hills, followed by island relaxation on the pristine white sands of Koh Rong.

Duration 7 Days / 6 Nights
Board Basis B&B + Selected Meals
Physical Pace Relaxed & Scenic
Transport Chauffeur & Speedboat
Detailed Schedule

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 01 Meals: Dinner {D}

Phnom Penh Arrival & Sunset River Promenade

Welcome at Phnom Penh International Airport by your private chauffeur and guide. Transfer to your boutique hotel. In the late afternoon, embark on a private wooden boat cruise along the confluence of the Tonle Sap and Mekong rivers, enjoying sunset over the capital.

Day 02 Meals: {B, L}

Phnom Penh to Kampot via Organic Pepper Plantation

Journey south by private air-conditioned vehicle through scenic rural villages. Visit an internationally certified organic Kampot pepper plantation to learn about the cultivation of black, red, and white pepper. Check in to your riverside lodge in Kampot.

Day 03 Meals: {B, L}

Bokor National Park Mountain Plateau

Drive up the winding road into the cool mist of Bokor National Park. Explore the historic French hill station, the Old Catholic Church, and the cliff edge with panoramic views stretching across the Gulf of Thailand and Vietnam's Phu Quoc island.

Day 04 Meals: {B, L, D}

Kep Crab Market & Rabbit Island Excursion

Short drive to the seaside town of Kep. Experience the lively morning crab market where local women haul baskets of fresh blue swimmer crabs directly from the sea. Board a local wooden boat to Koh Tonsay (Rabbit Island) for a peaceful beach day and freshly cooked seafood lunch.

Day 05 Meals: {B}

Speedboat to Koh Rong Tropical Island

Depart Kep along the scenic coastal highway to Sihanoukville pier. Board a modern scheduled catamaran speedboat to Koh Rong. Arrive at your beachfront resort and spend the afternoon swimming in crystal clear turquoise water.

Day 06 Meals: {B}

Koh Rong Leisure & Snorkeling

Full day at leisure on Koh Rong. Relax under coconut palms, kayak along secluded coves, or join a coral reef snorkeling excursion. Evening at leisure to enjoy beachfront dining.

Day 07 Meals: {B}

Catamaran Return & Express Highway to Phnom Penh

Morning catamaran transfer back to mainland Sihanoukville. Meet your private vehicle for a smooth transfer along the expressway back to Phnom Penh airport or your onward departure hotel.
